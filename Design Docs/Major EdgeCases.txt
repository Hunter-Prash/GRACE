Long answer (and a fun edge case): Because she reads the last 50 chat messages before responding, she "remembers" the milestones you recently created. However, if you created the goal 3 weeks ago, it won't be in her recent chat history!

If that happens, and you say "I finished the AWS module", she might guess the key wrong (e.g., she might try to update "aws-module" instead of "finish-module").

To fix that in the future, we would either:

Inject your active goals directly into her System Prompt.
Give her a get_goals tool so she can search the database for the correct key before she runs the update!
