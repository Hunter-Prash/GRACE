CALENDER USAGE

Read/Fetch Events (getCalendarEvents)

What it does: Pulls your schedule for any time period.
How to use it: "What does my schedule look like today?" or "Am I free next Tuesday afternoon?"
Schedule New Events (scheduleEvent)

What it does: Blocks time on your calendar for a new event. It supports descriptions and even recurring rules!
How to use it: "Block 2 hours for a deep dive on AWS tomorrow at 10 AM" or "Schedule a recurring team sync every Friday at 4 PM."
Reschedule Events (rescheduleEvent)

What it does: Moves an existing event to a new time block.
How to use it: "Move my AWS deep dive to tomorrow at 2 PM instead."
Cancel Events (cancelEvent)

What it does: Completely deletes an event from your calendar.
How to use it: "Cancel that team sync for this Friday."


EXPENSE USAGE
Log a New Expense (addTransaction)

What it does: Records a new transaction into your DynamoDB table. It automatically extracts the cost, maps it to one of your strict categories, and grabs the exact time.
How to use it: "I just spent 500 on food at a cafe" or "Log 2000 for my AWS hosting this month" or "Add an expense of 800 for a movie ticket."
Check Your Spending (getTransactions)

What it does: Pulls all your transaction data between any two dates so Grace can analyze it, give you totals, or break it down by category.
How to use it: "How much have I spent so far this month?", "What were my total expenses last week?", or "Give me a breakdown of all my travel expenses in June."
